02 Journey

Industry and academia

A path where professional software development, university studies and research gradually started to overlap.

2018 — 2021 · Software engineering

Started working in software while still in high school

I entered the software industry in 2018, working for a company while I was still completing high school. Those first years gave me early exposure to professional development, real-world constraints and the responsibility that comes with maintaining software used outside the classroom.

2020 — 2026 · University of Catania

BSc in Computer Science

I began my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in 2020. Alongside my studies, I continued working as a developer and gradually became more interested in machine learning and research-oriented problems. I completed the degree in 2026.

2021 — Present · Freelance software engineering

Working with different companies and projects

After leaving my first company in 2021, I continued working as a freelance Software Engineer for different companies. This has allowed me to work across different codebases, technical environments and project requirements while continuing my university studies.

2025 — Present · Research

From undergraduate studies to published research

Around 2025, during the final part of my Bachelor's degree, I began working more seriously on research. My interests developed around machine learning, generative models, synthetic data and multimedia forensics, eventually leading to published work while I was still an undergraduate student.

2026 — Present · University of Catania

MSc studies

After completing my Bachelor's degree in 2026, I started my Master's degree and continue to combine academic study, research projects and professional software engineering.
